
# Global Retail & Financial Performance Analysis 

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates advanced data analytics and visualization techniques using Microsoft Excel. I transformed a raw dataset of over 2,000 global transactions into a high-level executive dashboard. The analysis covers sales performance, salesperson efficiency, market share, and financial cash flow trends.

## Key Technical Achievements

* **Data Consolidation & Integrity**: Successfully merged **North America (NA)** and **Latin America (LATAM)** regions into a unified **"Americas"** entity to support organizational restructuring.
* **Financial Auditing**: Conducted a data audit to incorporate missing transactions from APAC and EMEA regions, ensuring 100% reporting accuracy after a legacy system failure.
* **Advanced Data Modeling**: Utilized Pivot Tables to break down revenue by item category and geographical region, applying complex filters to isolate high-value products like appliances.

## Data Visualizations

I implemented a variety of advanced charts to translate numbers into business strategy:

1. **Trend Analysis (Line Chart)**: Visualized monthly Revenue vs. Expenses to track profit margins from January to May.
2. **Performance Benchmarking (Column Chart)**: Compared unit sales across international team members, identifying top performers like Fahim Rahman.
3. **Market Composition (Doughnut Chart)**: Analyzed market share across Electronics, Fashion, Home Decor, and Groceries.
4. **Correlation Discovery (Scatter Plot)**: Established the relationship between advertising spend and monthly sales growth using exponential trendlines.
5. **Hierarchical Budgeting (Treemap)**: Mapped department-level budget allocations across IT, Marketing, and HR.
6. **Financial Bridge (Waterfall Chart)**: Built a "Starting-to-Ending" cash flow bridge to visualize the impact of sales, rent, and taxes.
7. **Sales Pipeline (Funnel Chart)**: Tracked customer conversion rates from initial website visits to closed sales.
